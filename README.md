🛍️ Supply Chain & Business Performance Analysis
E-Commerce | Beauty & Personal Care | End-to-End Analytics Project

📌 Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive supply chain and business performance analysis for a simulated e-commerce beauty & personal care brand.

The dataset was programmatically generated using Python, designed to replicate realistic operational and strategic business scenarios including:

Warehouse inventory movement

Courier performance variability

Discount impact on revenue

Customer segmentation behavior

Multi-state demand distribution

The objective was to uncover inefficiencies, optimize operations, and design strategic, data-driven business recommendations.

🧠 Business Problem Statement


E-commerce businesses often struggle with:

Inventory imbalance (overstock vs stock-outs)

Rising logistics costs

Courier SLA inconsistencies

Inefficient discounting strategies

Weak customer tier prioritization

This project simulates and solves these challenges using a structured analytics approach.
